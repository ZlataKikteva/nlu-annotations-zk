# Sentence interpretation task

## Task overview

You will see a short text from a wikiHow article, together with the article title. One sentence in the text, the target sentence, is shown in bold; the rest of the text and the article title are considered to be context. You will see a question about the target sentence and several answer options. 

In total, there are 15 questions to answer, which should take about 25-30 minutes.

Select one answer option that best matches what can be inferred from the sentence in the given context. Ask yourself: what did the writer most likely mean when they wrote the instruction?

More than one option may seem possible; choose the one that can most directly be inferred from the sentence in the given context. If none of the options is communicated by the sentence, select "None can be inferred from the target sentence".

The texts in this survey are unedited excerpts from wikiHow, so they may contain some grammatical or spelling errors. Do not let these affect your judgments.

### Example 1 

**Article:** Advertise Small Businesses

If you sell items, don't overlook the benefits of Instagram! You can post photos of your most popular or unique items. **Take photos of your work, inventory, or building.** Let your products or services speak for themselves! Whenever you complete a job, finish a new project, or get new inventory, snap a few well-lit photos. You can post your photos on social media, your website, or share them with potential customers as examples of your work.

Question: According to the target sentence and given the context, what should you use the photos of your work, inventory, or building for?

- For tracking inventory changes.
- ✅ For marketing purposes.
- For entering your shop in a beauty pageant.
- For planning future projects.
- None can be inferred from the target sentence.

"For marketing purposes" can most directly be inferred: the article is about advertising, and the text suggests posting the photos on social media or sharing them with potential customers. 

### Example 2

**Article:** Get Rid of Blackheads When Your Skin is Sensitive

Use a honey-cinnamon cleanser. Another natural cure some people have had luck with is a raw honey-cinnamon mixture. **Simply combine equal parts of raw honey and cinnamon powder.** Rub it on your face. Once you've thoroughly rubbed it in, cover it with a light cotton strip or a thick paper towel. Leave it for about 5 minutes before pulling the strip off and rinsing.

Question: According to the target sentence and given the context, what could you use in place of the powdered ingredient?

- A small amount of aloe vera gel.
- A few drops of cinnamon oil.
- A few drops of dragon tears.
- A few drops of tea tree oil.
- ✅ None can be inferred from the target sentence.

The text says to mix honey with cinnamon powder, but it does not mention anything that could replace the powder. Cinnamon oil may sound like a natural alternative, but that idea comes from general knowledge, not from the text.
